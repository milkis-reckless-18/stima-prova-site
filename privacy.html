Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

What we collect, why, and the rights you have over it. Consent-first is a product feature; it is also a privacy policy.

Data we collect

Account data: name, email, organization, and role when you register or contact us. Assessment data: tasks, rubrics, and role descriptions you submit; session recordings and logs produced during assessments, including prompts, edits, verification steps, model usage, and timing. Result data: scores, levels, credentials, and reviewer decisions.

We do not collect camera, microphone, or keystroke data, and we do not run lockdown browsers or background process monitoring.

How we use data

We use data to operate the Service: to run assessments, produce cited scores, sync grades to your LMS, and give participants their verified results. We use minimal operational telemetry to keep the Service secure and reliable, and we send service and product communications you can opt out of.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use customer content or participant data to train machine-learning models.

Session recording and consent

Sessions are recorded only inside the assessment workspace and only with informed consent, presented before the session begins. Participants see exactly what is recorded and what is never collected. Consent records are retained as part of the assessment evidence.

Workspace recordings are used solely to produce and explain scores and to let authorized reviewers verify them. A human confirms every outcome before it is finalized, and participants may dispute a result.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Instructors, institutions, and hiring teams can export and request deletion of their data; participants can request a copy of or deletion of their own results, subject to records an institution is legally required to retain.

To exercise any right, use the contact form on this site or write to info@mystima.io. We respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. Institutions remain responsible for providing required notices to students and candidates.

Security and retention

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, restrict access by role, and log administrative actions. Higher-education deployments run inside the customer's security perimeter where required, and we align our data handling with FERPA. WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility; HECVAT and SOC 2 documentation are in progress.

We retain assessment data for as long as an account is active or as required by contract and law, then delete or anonymize it. Backup copies are deleted on a rolling cycle.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions or requests: use the contact form on this site or write to info@mystima.io. Stima Prova, mystima.io.

We may update this policy from time to time; the revised version will be posted here with an updated date.
