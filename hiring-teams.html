How it works

Paste your role

One session

The candidate works one focused session with AI explicitly allowed, recorded with consent. No camera, no lockdown browser.

03

Two scores

The deliverable on your criteria, the session on the published scale. Every score cites a moment in the record.

What you get, per candidate

A decision you can defend.
Not a feeling.

Quality of the work

A 0–100 score on the finished deliverable, graded blind on your criteria, with the rubric beside it.

AI collaboration level

Delegator, Operator, or Director, read against what the task needed. This task needed a Director, and the session showed one.

Every claim cites the tape

Each score points to a moment in the recorded session, so anyone on your team can check the reasoning in one click.

Risk flags sit beside the grade

Unverified acceptance on high-stakes work and the blind pass get flagged, even when the numbers look fine. Compliance gets artifacts, not assurances.

Candidates get a fairer process

Shorter, AI-allowed, with a verified result they own and can share. You get the paper trail.
