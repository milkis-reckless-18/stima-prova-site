The AI-collaboration scale

Three levels.
Higher is not better.

The scale reads how much thinking was handed to the model, prompts, edits, verification, model switching, time. The grade is calibration: the observed level against what the task demanded.

Pick a task · the right level changes

This task calls for Operator Verifiable and routine, but the spec has teeth. Iterate against the requirements and spot-check the output.
Level 1

Delegator

Hands work to AI

Restates the brief, accepts the first answer. Right for verifiable, low-stakes work.

Level 2

Operator

Works with AI

Iterates, corrects, edits substantively. The right default for mixed, everyday work.

Level 3

Director

Leads the work

Sets the approach first, verifies the parts most likely wrong, owns the outcome.
